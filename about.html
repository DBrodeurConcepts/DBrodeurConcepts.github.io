About

No genre. No medium. No limit.

DBrodeur Concepts started as a way to make things without asking what category they belonged to first. A portrait doesn't have to be a painting. A record doesn't have to be the only thing worth drawing. If the idea calls for a canvas, it gets one. If it calls for a prompt and a render, it gets that instead.

Too much structure kills the thing that makes art worth making. So the work here doesn't sit in one lane — hip-hop culture next to quiet rooftop scenes, vintage tech next to something built entirely by hand. What ties it together is the eye it's made through, not the format it lands in.

Every piece is signed and original.
